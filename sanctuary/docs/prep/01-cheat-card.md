# 01 · Live cheat card

> Sanctuary finale prep. Front door and reading order: [`README.md`](README.md). Glance at this card backstage. Everything else is the deep version.

### Say this in 20 seconds
> "Sanctuary helps Peel decide which trusted buildings to harden first before the next heat wave or outage. We start with public heat-risk data, add real community buildings people already use, rank the first five candidates with a transparent score, and label what is verified, what is modelled, and what still needs a site audit. Our number one is Malton Community Centre and Library, a real building in Peel's top heat-vulnerability quintile."

### The line they remember
> "The safest building isn't the nearest building. It's the one people already trust, inside the heat-risk zone."

### The 10 you must nail (one line each)

| # | If they ask... | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is it? | "We rank the five trusted Peel buildings to harden first before the next heat wave or outage." |
| 2 | The problem? | "Heat risk and shelter access are both uneven. The most exposed people often can't reach a cool, powered place they trust." |
| 3 | Why not just a heat map? | "A heat map ends in a colour. We end in a decision: harden these five named buildings first." |
| 4 | Is the data real? | "Yes. The heat layer is Peel Public Health's public index, 282 census tracts. Anything we estimate, we label as estimated." |

| 7 | Why Malton? | "Real civic building, HVI quintile 5 (the highest), a community centre and library, inside the hottest pocket." |
| 8 | Who pays? | "The 15 percent Clean Electricity tax credit, the one tax-exempt owners can reach, plus the Green Municipal Fund and Save on Energy." |
| 9 | The impact? | "A pre-verify list. Before the next heat day, a planner already knows which trusted buildings to check first. We don't claim lives saved." |
| 10 | Why should this win? | "Original idea, verified public data with the receipts on screen, fits both sponsors, and ends in a decision a planner could act on Monday. It fakes nothing." |

### The one reframe that wins Q1 (memorize the spirit)
> "The point isn't to build new cooling centres. It's to harden buildings people already know, already trust, and can already reach. Harden a building people recognize and they'll actually go there when it matters. A new facility nobody recognizes doesn't solve the problem."

This is the 20 percent trust factor in plain words, backed by the Brampton Lighthouse Project.

### Honesty guardrails (never cross these)

| Don't say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "This building is already a hub." | "Candidate hub, not yet equipped." |
| "It has solar / batteries / backup power." | "Pending. A site-audit question." |
| "The battery is X kW / costs $Y." | "Planning estimate, requires a site audit." (No kW, kWh, or dollar figures.) |
| "Reachable population is exactly N." | "Modelled 500 metre catchment. Malton is about 5,900; the rest are pending." |
| "This will save lives / restore power." | "A planning layer. Impact needs the next steps." |
| "GridExchange ran in Peel." | "It ran in Vaughan, Markham, Barrie, Richmond Hill, and Hamilton. A template Peel can adopt." |
| "The 30 percent credit pays for it." | "The 15 percent Clean Electricity credit. The one tax-exempt owners can reach." |
| "AI-powered." | "AI drafts a checklist behind a no-overclaim filter. It doesn't make the ranking." |

**When unsure, fall back to:** *"This is a candidate hub and a planning estimate. It needs a site audit before anyone treats it as ready."*

### The three reflexes
1. **Lead with the decision, not the map.** "We rank the five Peel buildings to harden first."
2. **Label honesty out loud.** Verified, modelled, or pending, before they ask.
3. **Use proper nouns.** "Malton Community Centre and Library, 3540 Morning Star Drive," HVI quintile 5.

### Close with
> "Harden these five trusted buildings first, then repeat the same honest method across Alectra's service territory. That's Sanctuary."
