## Question priority — what to ask mentors first

1. **Open questions from canonical docs** ([`../../docs/seneca-hackathon-context.md`](../../docs/seneca-hackathon-context.md#open-questions)) — submission deadline conflict, required tech, team size, video length, rubric weights. Get these confirmed Day 1.
2. **Domain validation** for chosen idea — does the energy story hold up to expert scrutiny? If yes, that's a credibility win for the demo.
3. **Sponsor expectations** if a sponsor track is being chased — what does the sponsor actually want vs what we're building?

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Don't ask mentors questions Google can answer. Use them for taste, validation, sponsor-context.
- Don't pitch the idea cold. Frame: "We're building X for Theme Y. Our demo moment is Z. What would make this land harder for judges in this domain?"
- Don't argue with mentor feedback in the moment. Capture it in [[../50-build-log/decisions]] with our reasoning if we choose not to follow it.

## Logging mentor takeaways

After each mentor conversation, add to that day's [[../50-build-log/README|build log]]:
- Who (mentor name + domain)
- 3-bullet summary of what they said
- 1 action we'll take (or explicit "noted, not acting on it because…")
