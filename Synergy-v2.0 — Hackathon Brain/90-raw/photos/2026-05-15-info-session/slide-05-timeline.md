## Important note — submission-deadline conflict

This slide says **"Final Submission Deadline" on May 26th**.

The hackathon **website** (<https://www.senecahackathon.com/>) says Phase 1 runs May 24–28 with submissions due **Thursday May 28**.

These two sources disagree by 2 days. The conflict is logged as Open Question #1 in [`../../../../docs/seneca-hackathon-context.md`](../../../../docs/seneca-hackathon-context.md#open-questions).

**Current decision:** treating May 28 (website) as canonical for `event.yaml` / build planning. Contingency plan if organizers correct to May 26 is documented in [`../../../../docs/timeline.md`](../../../../docs/timeline.md#what-changes-if-the-deadline-is-actually-2026-05-26-photo-version).

This photo is the EVIDENCE behind the May 26 reading. **Don't lose it.** If the deadline conflict gets resolved against us at kickoff, this slide is what we cite to organizers.
