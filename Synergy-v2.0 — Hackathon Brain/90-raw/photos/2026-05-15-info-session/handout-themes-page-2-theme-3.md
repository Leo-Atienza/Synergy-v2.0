## Verbatim text — Theme 3

> ## Theme 3: Community Energy, Equity and Sustainability
>
> Not all communities' experience energy and climate challenges in the same way. Some face higher costs, more outages, or greater exposure to extreme weather. This theme focuses on fairness and community well-being. Students can explore how data and maps can help show differences between neighborhoods, highlight community needs, or support planning for a more sustainable and inclusive future. The goal is to create clear and accessible ideas that help decision makers understand where support or investment can make the biggest difference.
>
> **Sample example:** Students might explore how different communities experience energy costs or climate impacts and create a visual that helps identify where support or investment could make a positive difference. The goal would be to highlight community needs in a clear and accessible way.

## Notes on transcription

- Page heading "Theme 3: Community Energy, Equity and Sustainability" — uses **comma between "Energy" and "Equity"**, then **"and"** before "Sustainability" (no Oxford comma between "Equity" and "and"). Matches handout exactly.
- **Original-grammar quirk preserved:** the first sentence reads `"Not all communities' experience energy and climate challenges in the same way."` — the apostrophe after "communities" is in an unusual position grammatically (likely a typo for `"Not all communities experience…"` without the apostrophe, OR `"Not all communities' experiences with energy and climate challenges are the same"` — but neither is what's printed). Transcribed verbatim; do NOT silently correct.
- Two phrases in this theme are likely judge-rubric signals: **"fairness and community well-being"** (impact axis) and **"help decision makers understand"** (communication axis, decision-maker persona).

## Why this theme matters strategically

Theme 3 is the **broadest** of the three. From [[../../../10-event/judge-rubric-thinking]]:

- "Help decision makers understand" framing → user persona is a planner/policymaker, UI should look dashboard-like
- "Fairness and community well-being" → impact axis is heavily weighted
- Easier to pivot scope mid-build without breaking theme alignment than the more constrained Themes 1 & 2

If the May 24 challenge unveil narrows things, Theme 3 has the most room to absorb the constraint.
