## The five answers

**1. "You only do heat." -> We now show three hazards.**
Heat (verified HVI), flood (TRCA regulatory floodplain), and winter / energy-burden (ON-Marg 2021), each separately toggleable, each honestly labelled. **TRCA itself publishes a combined "Flood and Heat Vulnerable Areas in Peel" service** — an authoritative body already treats flood + heat as overlapping in Peel. Our multi-hazard siting extends that.

**2. "Where's the winter cold map?" -> We deliberately don't fake one.**
This is the strongest answer. *"Heat vulnerability varies block to block because of the urban heat island, so we map it per tract. Winter cold does not concentrate that way; there's no winter heat-island. A per-tract cold-temperature index would invent a gradient that isn't real, so we refuse to. Winter resilience need is driven by energy affordability and marginalization, which IS real per-area data, and we map that with the 2021 Ontario Marginalization Index, labelled modelled."* This pre-empts the sharpest data-judge question and shows method literacy.

**3. "Who opens this Monday morning?" -> Three named, real desks.**
- **Region of Peel, Office of Climate Change and Energy Management** — prioritizes which trusted buildings to harden first.
- **Region of Peel, Regional Emergency Management** — prepares and opens public buildings during an extreme-heat day or an outage. (Peel Public Health built the HVI to find where need is highest.)
- **Alectra Utilities, distribution planning** — where hardened solar-and-battery sites strengthen the local grid as distributed energy resources, the kind of resilience in its distribution-system plan.

**4. "Where did the weights come from?" -> Team judgment, structured on Peel's own logic.**
The 35 / 25 / 20 / 10 / 10 model is our reasoned judgment, structured on Peel's HVI sub-scores (exposure / sensitivity / adaptive capacity) and the USDN resilience-hub functions, both already cited. Heat leads at 35 because it is the only fully verified, spatially-resolved axis. No external-framework claim.

**5. "Is any number real?" -> Yes, and we show the receipts.**
- **Malton now carries a real, reproducible catchment estimate: ~5,900 people** within 500 m (sum of the 2021 populations of the seven dissemination areas whose StatCan representative point falls inside the circle). Honest about being modelled, and we state the boundary sensitivity (~4,500-5,900 depending on how DA centres are defined). Beside it, a **verified** anchor: the census tract holds **5,217** people (2021).
- Populations were **cross-checked against two independent StatCan-derived sources** and matched exactly.
